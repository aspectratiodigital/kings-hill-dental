About us.

We have been the principal dentists at our family owned dentist surgery since 2009, with over 20 years of experience each. Our passion and dedication to dentistry has seen the practice grow and expand and we are deeply thankful for the wonderful environment, team and patients that surrounds us.

We are a dentistry focused solely on your interests and we endeavour to provide our patients with the best quality of care and advice, ensuring you can make the most informed decision about the dental work you're looking for. We're aiming to push back against the wave of cosmetic-first dentistry that is plastered all over social media by providing our patients with informed, professional advice and guidance to help assess the best option for each individual patient, for both dental care and cosmetic work. We believe the best smile is the one that you want to show off and we work to build that smile in the least invasive and most caring way possible.

​

With our well equipped, state-of-the-art surgeries we are able offer a wide range of general and preventative dentistry options, as well as specialist treatments such as orthodontics and implants. We also provide premium patient-oriented cosmetic treatment and skin care.
